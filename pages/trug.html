Retail Investor Awareness Strategy and 2026 Growth Roadmap

This page outlines Trugolf Holdings Inc (TRUG)' 2026 retail investor awareness strategy, detailing how visibility, engagement, and sustained investor interest are built across digital distribution channels.

Why Retail Investor Awareness Matters for Microcap Companies

Microcap public companies operate in a fundamentally different environment than large cap issuers:

  • Limited institutional coverage
  • Minimal Wall Street analyst exposure
  • Lower daily trading volume
  • Higher volatility driven by news flow

Retail investor awareness directly impacts:

  • Daily trading liquidity
  • Shareholder diversity
  • Price discovery efficiency
  • Market reaction to press releases and milestones
  • Ability to raise capital at stronger valuations

"Visibility does not create value on its own, but lack of visibility suppresses value."

What We Do

We build and execute multi-channel retail investor awareness campaigns designed to consistently place Trugolf Holdings Inc (TRUG) in front of active market participants who already invest in microcap equities.

Our role is not to replace investor relations, but to digitally amplify IR-approved messaging at scale, ensuring it reaches qualified retail investors.

2026 Retail Awareness Roadmap

PHASE 1 Q1 2026

Foundation and Audience Build

  • Expansion of proprietary retail investor databases
  • Segmentation by interest, microcap focus, and trading behavior
  • Messaging alignment with corporate milestones and IR guidance
  • Optimization of investor landing pages for education and engagement

Objective: Ensure every future announcement reaches a qualified, receptive, and repeat investor audience.

PHASE 2 Q1 to Q4 2026

Press Release Amplification Engine

  • Distribution to existing investor databases across TopStocks, Stock Wire, BigStocks
  • Additional owned and partner investor networks
  • Delivery to investors already engaged in public equities
  • Re-circulation of key press releases over multiple days to maximize exposure

Objective: Press releases are actively seen and engaged with, not simply published.

PHASE 3Q2 to Q4 2026

Paid On-Demand Investor Advertising

  • Programmatic display advertising
  • Native advertising placements
  • Financial content networks
  • Focused traffic sources

Objective: Convert passive exposure into informed investor awareness.

PHASE 4 Ongoing

Social and Financial Platform Distribution

  • StockTwits distribution to small cap audiences
  • MarketBeat exposure for investor discovery
  • X distribution through finance focused accounts and networks
  • Organic reposting across multiple investor communities

Objective: Maintain consistent visibility within daily investor conversations.

PHASE 5 Ongoing

Traditional IR Through Personal Investor Networks

  • Personal networks of active retail investors
  • Family office contacts
  • High net worth trading groups
  • Microcap focused investor communities

Objective: Add credibility and trust, reinforcing digital exposure with direct investor relationships.
